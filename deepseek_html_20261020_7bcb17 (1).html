The Man Behind The Experience

A Career Defined by Service, Engineering and Leadership

Born on 10 March 1952, Brigadier General (Retd.) Engr. M Mofizur Rahman was commissioned into the Corps of Engineers on 21 June 1970. He served in the Bangladesh Army, rising to the rank of Brigadier General before retiring from active military service on 21 January 2001.

Following his military career, he transitioned into executive civilian administration, serving as Executive Chairman of the Bangladesh Export Processing Zones Authority (BEPZA) from 2002 to 2004. He also served as Managing Director of Dhaka Electric Supply Company Limited (DESCO) and West Zone Power Distribution Company Limited (WZPDCL).

In his post-public career, he established himself as a pioneering management consultant, heading R&G Consulting as Chief Executive Officer and Lead Consultant, while championing global governance, compliance, and international accreditation.

Throughout a career spanning more than five decades, he has consistently applied disciplined systems thinking, quantitative engineering principles, and contemporary organizational practices to national public-sector enterprises and modern industrial institutions. He holds an MS in Renewable Energy Technology from the University of Dhaka, an MBA from Bangladesh Open University, and a B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from BUET.
